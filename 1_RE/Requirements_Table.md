# Requirements Table

**Problem Statement #16 — Remote Patient Vitals Alert & Monitoring App**
Lab 1: Requirements Engineering & UML Use-Case Modelling

| Req ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FR-001** | Functional | The system shall continuously evaluate ingested patient vitals against configured clinical thresholds and flag critical anomalies within 2 seconds. | High | **Pass:** High heart rate (>140 BPM) triggers immediate escalation alert. **Fail:** Metric spike ignored or delayed > 5s. | Given (baseline requirement). |
| **FR-002** | Functional | The system shall escalate an unacknowledged critical alert to the next tier of the on-call caregiver matrix if it is not acknowledged within 3 minutes of being raised. | High | **Pass:** A simulated unacknowledged alert is automatically re-routed to Tier-2 caregiver at exactly 3 min, logged with timestamp. **Fail:** Alert remains only with the original caregiver beyond 3 min 30s. | Ensures a missed alert never goes unnoticed and reaches an available caregiver in time-critical post-operative situations. |
| **FR-003** | Functional | The system shall allow an On-Call Caregiver to acknowledge an active alert, recording the caregiver ID and acknowledgment timestamp against the alert. | High | **Pass:** Tapping "Acknowledge" stops further escalation and stores caregiver ID + timestamp in the alert log within 1s. **Fail:** Alert continues to escalate after acknowledgment, or acknowledgment is not logged. | Provides an auditable chain of custody for every emergency alert, required for clinical accountability. |
| **FR-004** | Functional | The system shall allow an authorized System Administrator to configure and update per-patient clinical thresholds for SpO2, Heart Rate, and Blood Pressure. | Medium | **Pass:** Updated threshold values are saved, version-stamped, and applied to the next incoming telemetry reading within 10s. **Fail:** Old threshold values are still used after an update, or the change is not saved. | Post-operative patients have individualized clinical baselines, so thresholds cannot be one-size-fits-all. |
| **FR-005** | Functional | The system shall automatically notify the Emergency Dispatch Service when an escalated alert remains unacknowledged after the final caregiver tier's timeout has elapsed. | High | **Pass:** If no caregiver acknowledges within the final tier's timeout, an emergency dispatch request with patient location and vitals snapshot is transmitted within 5s. **Fail:** No dispatch notification is sent, or it is sent before all caregiver tiers have timed out. | Acts as the last line of defense to guarantee patient safety when the human caregiver chain fails to respond. |
| **NFR-001** | Nonfunctional | The telemetry ingestion gateway shall support at least 500 concurrent continuous telemetry streams with 99.99% uptime. | High | **Pass:** Benchmarking tests confirm target latency and security standards under simulated peak load. | Given (baseline requirement). |
| **NFR-002** | Nonfunctional | The system shall encrypt all patient vitals data, both in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), and shall comply with HIPAA data-protection requirements. | High | **Pass:** Security audit / penetration test confirms no vitals data is transmitted or stored unencrypted, and access logs show role-based access control. **Fail:** Any patient data field is found in plaintext in transit or at rest. | Vitals and location data are sensitive PHI; a breach directly threatens patient privacy and regulatory compliance (Type: Security & Compliance). |

*Note: FR-001 and NFR-001 were provided in the problem statement; FR-002–FR-005 and NFR-002 were authored for this lab.*
